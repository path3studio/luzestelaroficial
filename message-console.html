Luz Estelar — Message Console

Internal tool: our team reads and answers Instagram Direct messages sent to the Luz Estelar business account.
Step 1Log in with Meta
Step 2Select the business asset
Step 3Open a conversation (sender profile)
Step 4Reply from this console

1. Log in with Meta

Uses Facebook Login. The console requests: pages_show_list, pages_messaging, pages_manage_metadata, instagram_basic, instagram_manage_messages.

2. Select the business asset (Facebook Page ↔ Instagram account)

Only Pages you manage are listed. Selecting one loads its connected Instagram professional account.

3. Instagram conversations

4. Reply from this console

After sending, the same message appears in the Instagram app of the person you replied to. Production replies run server-to-server with the same Page permissions; this console is the human-operated version of that flow.
